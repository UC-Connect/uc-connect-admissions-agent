# UC Transfer Admission Guarantee (TAG)

TAG provides guaranteed admission for qualifying transfer applicants at six participating UC campuses, provided all shared and campus-specific requirements are met.

A student may submit a TAG application to one participating UC campus. The student must also submit the regular UC application for admission.

TAG eligibility, GPA thresholds, required coursework, excluded majors, and deadlines vary by campus and application cycle. The official TAG matrix should be checked for the relevant year.

The six-campus statement does not mean every major at every participating campus is eligible.

## Answer guardrails

- Do not claim UC Berkeley, UCLA, or UC San Diego participates in TAG.
- Do not promise TAG eligibility without checking the current TAG matrix.
- Do not infer eligibility only from GPA.
- Always tell users to verify campus-specific major restrictions and deadlines.
