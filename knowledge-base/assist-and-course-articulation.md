# ASSIST and Course Articulation

ASSIST is California's official source for articulation and transferability information among California public colleges and universities.

California community college students can use ASSIST to review:
- UC-transferable course lists;
- campus and major articulation agreements;
- course-to-course equivalencies;
- major preparation agreements, when available.

Students must select the correct sending institution, receiving institution, academic year, and major or department.

An articulation agreement for one academic year, campus, college, or major should not be treated as universal.

## Answer guardrails

- Never invent a course equivalency.
- Ask for the student's community college, target UC campus, target major, and applicable academic year before interpreting an agreement.
- If ASSIST shows “no course articulated,” do not automatically interpret that as the requirement being waived.
