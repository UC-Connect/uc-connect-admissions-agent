# UC Transfer Dates and Deadlines

UC application dates can change by admission term and cycle. The official UC transfer dates and deadlines page is the controlling source.

For the fall 2027 cycle, the UC Admissions homepage indicates:
- the application opens August 1, 2026;
- the fall 2027 submission period is October 1 through November 30, 2026.
- the Transfer Admission Guarantee (TAG) application filing period for fall 2027 is September 1-30, 2026;
- the Transfer Academic Update (TAU) opens December 15, 2026, with a priority deadline of January 31, 2027;
- fall 2027 admission decision notifications begin March 1, 2027 and continue through May 1, 2027;
- the Statement of Intent to Register deadline for admitted fall 2027 transfer students is June 1, 2027;
- final official transcripts are due July 1, 2027;
- official AP, IB, and other documents such as IGETC or Cal-GETC certification are due July 15, 2027.

Winter and spring admission are offered only by selected campuses and programs, and their filing periods may differ.

TAG has a separate application and filing period from the regular UC application.

## Answer guardrails

- Always state the exact admission term and calendar year.
- Never reuse a prior cycle's deadline without checking the official page.
- Distinguish “application opens” from “application submission period.”
- A TAG application does not replace the regular UC application.
- Do not apply fall 2027 dates to other admission terms unless the retrieved context states those dates.
