# UC Transfer Basic Requirements

For junior-level transfer eligibility, UC states that applicants generally must complete at least 60 semester units or 90 quarter units of UC-transferable college credit.

The minimum UC-transferable GPA is generally:
- 2.4 for California residents;
- 2.8 for nonresidents.

Some campuses and selective majors require substantially higher GPAs.

No more than 14 semester units or 21 quarter units of the required transferable credit may generally be taken Pass/No Pass or Credit/No Credit, subject to UC rules and stated exceptions.

Applicants must also complete UC's required transferable course pattern. Campus, college, and major preparation requirements may be additional.

## Answer guardrails

- Never describe the minimum GPA as a competitive GPA.
- Always distinguish minimum UC eligibility from campus or major selection requirements.
- Do not confirm that a specific course transfers without checking ASSIST or an official campus evaluation.
